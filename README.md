### A Villager overhaul mod for Minecraft

Social contract is a mod designed to add more depth to 
Minecraft by making Villagers a bit more intelligent, human, and
fun, while also tweaking the game to make Villagers less likely to get themselves killed.

This is my first time attempting to mod Minecraft, so, there are bound to be many things that 
I didn't implement quite well. If you see anything you think is wrong, a Github Issue would be greatly 
appreciated.

<hr>

## Features:

Villagers will know how large their rooms are, and if they are safe from Monsters.

Villagers will now have a Happiness value that will dictate many parts of their behaviour.

Happiness represents how satisfied a Villager is with its circumstances. Things like having a safe home,
receiving gifts, not being hurt, and trading with players will be reflected in a Villagers Happiness score. 


Villagers that are high on Happiness will offer discounts on trades, as well as accept requests
for custom trades. Villagers that are low on Happiness will restrict their trades, or worse...

Players can request that a Villager follow them, but the Villager will only accept if it feels like it.

Villagers will try to survive a bit harder. They will no longer intentionally get stuck in 1x2x1 holes,
and will no longer wander around too far from its home, if it has one. Villagers will try
to stay near light sources. At night, a Villager with a room will close all the doors attached to its room.

Villagers that have reached the max happiness tier will obtain a trade for a 'Villager Totem'

A new Villager themes light block has been added to the game. This block, called the 'Villager Light', will burn and inflict slowness on zombies near it. 
However, it will gradually wither away over time. Villagers that come by withered lights will re-light them. This light can be
crafted with 9 iron ingots, and a Villager Totem.

See [Full Features](FullFeatures.md) for a complete list of added features.
